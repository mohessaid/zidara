# Video: product films and lesson videos

Distilled from the technical-video-script skill. Tools are chosen per job (screen recording
of the real app, a motion tool, an openly licensed clip); none is the default.

## A product film

At most 2 minutes. If there is a tangible product, demo it: viewers judge whether it
functions as claimed.

1. **Promise**, in one sentence, with no "and": one kernel, and a teacher's switch of
   framework redraws the same learner's path.
2. **Hook**, written first, in the first 30 seconds: the framework switch on a real phone.
3. **Beats**, one claim each, about 20 seconds each, in a two-column table:

   | Visual (what is on screen) | Narration |
   |---|---|
   | real app footage or a diagram | one sentence per idea |

   Claims in order: one kernel, many pedagogies (the switch) → offline learning, then sync
   → the governed agent (a queued answer, the teacher accepts, undo) → proof (the gauntlet
   numbers on screen) → who it serves, and the ask.
4. **Runtime budget**: narration words ÷ the speaker's measured rate (about 140 words a
   minute), plus the dead time for each beat. Total ≤ 115 s.
5. **Audio-only pass**: read the narration with the screen off. Rewrite every sentence that
   only makes sense with the picture ("as you can see here").
6. **Off screen**: other clients and projects, real children's names or faces,
   API keys, internal hostnames.
7. Captions are burned in for silent autoplay.

## Lesson videos (inside the product)

- Length ≤ 60 s, one idea, and the narration language matches the learner's locale.
- Captions in each language; the visuals must work without the sound.
- Licence must allow reuse in a free product (CC BY or CC0, or made by us). Record the source
  and licence in `content/CREDITS.md`.
- Size budget: ≤ 4 MB per minute at 720p (H.264, or AV1 where supported), so the offline pack
  stays small.
