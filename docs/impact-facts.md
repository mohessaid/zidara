## 1. Learning poverty (World Bank / UNESCO UIS)

Definition: the share of 10-year-olds who cannot read and understand a short, age-appropriate
text, adjusted for children out of school. It measures reading, not maths.

Regional rates are taken from the 2022 report's Annex 4 (low- and middle-income countries only).
The World Bank API now groups the region as "Middle East, North Africa, Afghanistan & Pakistan",
which is a different aggregate, so do not mix the two.
