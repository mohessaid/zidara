# Credits and licences

Every asset in a lesson pack is listed here with its source and licence. Nothing is used whose
licence does not allow a free product for children.

| Asset | Source | Licence |
|---|---|---|
| `lessons/equivalent-fractions.json` (text, questions, all versions, transcripts) | Written for Zidara; fraction answers checked by script | Zidara project |
| `web/public/media/fractions-intro.{ar,fr,en}.mp4` ("What is a fraction?", 36 s, no sound) | Drawn from code: `tools/video/fractions.html`, rendered by `tools/video/render.mjs` (Chromium + ffmpeg/x264) | Zidara project |

## Not used, and why

- **Piper's Arabic voice (`ar_JO-kareem`)**: its training data repository states no licence, so
  the rights to the voice are unknown (checked 16 October 2026). Narration waits for a voice
  with clear rights: the owner's own recording, or a licensed text-to-speech voice.
