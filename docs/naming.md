# Why "Zidara"

Named with David Placek's method (Lexicon Branding: Azure, Pentium, Swiffer, BlackBerry,
Sonos, Vercel). His rules: never describe, communicate an original idea, build for trust and
imagination, be easy to process, let sound carry meaning, and treat a name that divides the
team as a sign of strength.

## The Diamond questions

| Question | Answer |
|---|---|
| What does winning look like? | Every learner, on any connection and in any language, learning under a pedagogy that fits them, with teachers amplified by AI that stays inside rules people approve. |
| What do we have to win? | Pedagogy as a first-class part of the system (one kernel, many frameworks, many experiences), governed AI agents, native and offline-first apps, and a builder who knows the communities firsthand. |
| What do we need to win? | A working prototype, evidence from real learners, and partners in the settings we serve. |
| What do we need to say? | Knowledge that keeps growing, at home wherever the learner is. |

## The name

**Zidara** (zee-DAH-rah), an invented word built from familiar parts:
- **zid** (زِد): Arabic for "increase, give more", as in *rabbi zidni ilma*, "my Lord, increase
  me in knowledge". Knowledge that keeps growing is the original idea, said without
  describing a product.
- **dar** (دار): Arabic for "house" or "home", also the root of traditional places of learning.
  A home for learning, wherever the learner is.
- **-a:** an open ending that reads as a place and works in Arabic, French and English.

## Sound

- **Z** opens with energy and newness (Placek's Azure).
- **i** is light and quick; **a** is open and wide, a global reach.
- **d** and **r** are stable, familiar consonants.
- Three syllables, six letters, one obvious way to say and spell it.

## Linguistic check

- Every sound exists in Arabic, French and English. There is no **p**, **v** or **x**: Arabic
  has no p or v.
- No negative meaning found in Arabic, French or English. To check further: Berber
  (Tamazight), Turkish, Persian, Urdu, Malay/Indonesian, Swahili.
- Nearest well-known brand: Zara (fashion), a different field.

## Still to do before it is final

- Trademark searches (USPTO, EUIPO, WIPO) in education and software classes.
- Domains: `zidara.org`, `zidara.app`, `zidara.com`.
- Placek tests names with the people they are for. Say it to learners, teachers and parents in
  two or three communities and note what they feel.

## Other candidates, if Zidara divides the team the wrong way

- **Bazira:** "B" for reliability (Placek's BlackBerry finding); *baṣīra* (بصيرة) means
  "insight" in Arabic.
- **Nadira:** *nādira*, "rare, precious"; soft and warm, but also a common given name.
- **Zidni:** the most direct reference to the prayer, which some may find too religious for a
  global brand.
